About

Building financial foundations that scale.

Alejandro Franco is the CFO at Cairn Real Estate Holdings, specializing in strategic accounting, financial management, forecasting, and mergers and acquisitions. Before joining Cairn, Alejandro held a similar role at the subsidiary company, JPAR Real Estate, where he consistently exceeded expectations and focused on improving departmental workflows. His leadership resulted in significant gains in efficiency and operational processes.

As a second-generation Hispanic, Alejandro understands the challenges of education and opportunity within the Hispanic community. He is committed to driving progress and expanding opportunities. Alejandro is also actively involved in NAHREP, having served on the Regional Corporate Board of Governors and as a Board Director for the Plano/Garland chapter, where he promotes Hispanic homeownership and elevates industry leaders nationwide.
